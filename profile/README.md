# Twinspire Neural Solutions

Twinspire Neural Solutions is an AI-powered decision-support platform for
professional sport. It builds continuously updated neuromuscular and
physiological digital twins of individual athletes, unifying fragmented
performance and physiological signals into a single, objective picture that
helps reduce the rate of reinjury.

Learn more at [twinspire.ai](https://twinspire.ai) or connect on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/twinspire-neural-solutions).
